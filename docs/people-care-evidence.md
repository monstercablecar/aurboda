# Temporary care catalog: evidence and review

Reviewed November 12, 2026. This is an official-source check and contextual empathy review by the implementation agents, **not independent clinical review**. The implementation adds 54 general temporary-care records, 47 pregnancy/postpartum records, 44 source entries and 10 optional discussion milestones. The existing 142 records and 42 sources remain intact: the resulting catalog has 243 records and 86 source entries. Counts describe scope, not completeness or clinical quality.

The authoritative copy, predicates, source IDs and per-record review notes live in `apps/backend/src/services/people-catalog/care.ts` and `pregnancy.ts`. Existing age/relationship rules still apply. The help tree shows the same definitions that server matching evaluates. No temporary status is inferred from notes, gender, symptoms or relationships.

## Evidence interpretation

Pregnancy uses 16 ACOG pages as its principal clinical references. Their official indexed excerpts were checked, but direct page retrieval returned HTTP 402. Each source therefore explicitly records `official_indexed_excerpt`; it does not claim a full-page check. Eleven supplemental pregnancy sources were directly retrieved from CDC, FDA, NLM/MedlinePlus, NIMH, NHS and GOV.UK. Other temporary-care sources retain the root researcher's documented provenance.

Clinical education is distinguished from authored practical and ethical adaptations. A source can inform an offer without proving that exact household script clinically effective. Empathy notes consider the actual action, relationship, age and ability to decline; they do not certify a clinical intervention. Publisher dates are retained only where shown, with partial dates kept partial in source limitations. `last_verified` means this source check, not the publisher's medical review date.

UK and US sources describe different systems. UK employment material supports a practical discussion about work difficulties, not a universal claim about legal rights. The NHS child-bereavement page has an older stated review date; it was checked for this implementation but is not represented as newly clinically reviewed. No hotline phone numbers, medicine doses, diagnostic conclusions or procedure-specific recovery schedules are supplied.

## Pregnancy and postpartum boundaries

- Confirmation and general support remain useful without a gestational anchor. Optional windows require known gestational timing, even if a phase is manually selected. Phases on windowed entries also support accurate help-tree browsing.
- Testing entries are discussion opportunities, not obligations. RSV eligibility additionally depends on season, location and vaccine history. GBS uses the CDC-specific window; ACOG's broader FAQ wording is not presented as exact agreement. Professional vaccine recommendations can differ.
- Pregnancy never automatically changes to postpartum at the estimated due date. Birth preparation remains available beyond that estimate. Outcomes and transitions require explicit input.
- Loss support requires an explicit postpartum `loss_support` phase. Breastfeeding content excludes that phase and is conditional on breastfeeding; it neither assumes an infant outcome nor prescribes a feeding choice.
- The pregnant person may be a child, parent, spouse, lover or friend relative to the user. Adult-child guidance addresses the user as their parent. Separate minor-child guidance supports professional access without inventing consent law. No pregnancy record filters by gender.
- Fetal-development material is an optional educational resource, not a prediction about an individual fetus. Existing-child preparation is conditional on already parenting another child.
- High-risk pregnancy treatment, infertility care, diagnostic interpretation, detailed delivery protocols and legal decision-making are intentionally outside this catalog. Individual clinical advice takes precedence.

## Temporary-care boundaries

All eight status kinds have meaningful guidance. Illness, depression, anxiety, surgery and mourning include practical offers, relevant clinical escalation, role/age-specific support and selected overlaps. Grief phases are contexts, not mandatory stages. Adult agency and children's growing independence remain general relationship guidance; they do not presume a caregiving need. A logged support request is revisited rather than treated as permanent permission.

The source budget check aggregates catalog titles, summaries, actions and milestone copy by canonical URL, including the care-ethics panel. All are below 200 words per URL in this catalog; no new verbatim quotations are used. Review notes describe authorial ethical checks rather than reproducing source content. The exact evidence mappings below avoid duplicating the guidance prose.

## Validation

Focused catalog validation covers schema compatibility, unique IDs, references, all eight statuses, review metadata, source-specific pregnancy windows, phase browsing, open-ended birth preparation, explicit loss support, gender independence and adult/minor relationship direction. Broader rule, lifecycle, API and UI behavior is validated by their respective implementation suites; catalog tests alone do not prove those layers.
