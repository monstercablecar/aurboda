## Previously expanded concepts (also deferred)

- **Daily check-in:** one short question at a time; optional branches; unknown/skip answers; resumable drafts; save through existing domain services; avoid duplicate retries or inferred relationship changes.
- **Immediate feedback:** factual confirmation only after save success, a relevant updated view, correction action, optional animation respecting reduced motion. Recognition independent of whether an answer is positive or difficult.
- **Discovery cards:** begin with descriptive summaries, then adequately supported comparisons; explicit calculations and source records; AI wording cannot invent results; user usefulness feedback and topic suppression.
- **Weekly recap:** goals, observations, memories, changes and optional next focus; linked supporting entries; sensitive topics configurable; sharing requires a preview.
- **Fast capture:** consistent Quick add, pinned actions, contextual defaults, reusable templates; account-isolated drafts/queues and duplicate prevention; distinguish pending from saved. Later widgets/voice require separate design.

Suggested sequence was fast capture → check-in → feedback → recap → discoveries. It was not accepted. Proposed evaluation: retention, completion of chosen tracking plans, insight usefulness and capture effort; also duplicates, dismissals and user burden. Raw entry counts or app opens alone are inadequate success measures.

## Research used for ideation

These studies motivate hypotheses; they do not establish effectiveness of the proposed Aurboda features.

- [Social-media reward learning](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7910435/): observational analyses plus a controlled social-reward experiment.
- [Reward structures and accurate sharing](https://www.pnas.org/doi/10.1073/pnas.2216614120): platform incentives can shape sharing habits; application to data quality is an inference.
- [STEP UP trial](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6735420/): gamification/social incentives for physical activity, not proof of long-term health-app retention.
- [Drink Less notification trial](https://mhealth.jmir.org/2023/1/e38342/): near-term app openings do not by themselves establish sustained value.
