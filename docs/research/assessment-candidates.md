# Assessment candidates for Aurboda

Source review: November 18, 2026. This comparison is product research, not clinical advice or a recommendation to screen every user. “Repeat” is a suggested optional product cadence unless a source explicitly specifies one; it is not a prescription. Minute estimates are approximate planning estimates, not validated completion-time promises. Evidence applies to the studied population and instrument version, not automatically to this app. All entries remain unmonitored personal records.

## Selection

Start with **GAD-7** (anxiety symptoms) and **PEG** (pain and interference). They address different questions with relatively little work, have simple auditable scoring, and have accessible redistribution terms. Keep **WHO-5** in the existing external-result catalog: it contributes positive well-being, rather than another symptom total, but the current WHO publication carries noncommercial/share-alike conditions. Do not silently put that text under this repository's AGPL license. A physical-assessment entry point is justified by PEG and custom function observations; it should render the same library component as mental assessments.

**PHQ-9** is useful for depressive symptoms and much better supported than inventing a depression score. Defer in-app administration until a reviewed response flow exists for its self-harm item. A disclaimer alone is insufficient. **QIDS-SR16** is not a necessary companion to PHQ-9: it measures overlapping depressive symptoms, takes longer, has domain-maximum scoring, and its owner now routes proprietary permissions through Mapi. It could later be justified for a person already using it with their clinician, after permission and safety review. Neither belongs automatically in every treatment plan.

A person interested in one concrete activity may benefit more from an unscored custom question and a note than a battery of scales. The existing brief treatment check-ins remain unscored. Declining a deeper instrument must create no scheduled entry. Scores over a week, fortnight, or month cannot describe an immediate treatment effect.

## Overlap and implementation rules

- Depression: select PHQ-9 **or** QIDS for a specific need; adding both routinely is poor value. DASS also overlaps these and GAD-7. WHO-5 contributes a positive well-being perspective and does not replace safety assessment.
- Pain/function: PEG and PROMIS Pain Interference overlap. PSFS asks about personally meaningful activities; LEFS/QuickDASH are regional alternatives. WHODAS, RAND-36 and EQ-5D broaden scope but should not all be assigned.
- Sleep/fatigue: ISI, PSQI and PROMIS Fatigue answer different questions, yet are correlated and can create redundant work alongside sleep data. Symptoms and device measurements should not be treated as interchangeable.
- Standardized definitions are immutable and separately identified. Custom copies lose official scoring and validation claims, even when some wording remains. No diagnosis, treatment recommendation, or “treatment effectiveness” label is inferred.
- Preserve assessed time, recorded time, timezone, recall instructions, definition version, missing items and raw answers. No total is computed for an incomplete initial instrument. Skipping is not a zero. No imputation or extrapolation to unrecorded days.
- An appointment summary is a user-selected local download with a preview. It is not sent to anyone automatically. Compare only compatible versions and disclose overlapping recall, selection gaps and unmonitored status.
- A future PHQ-9/QIDS release must include a non-alarmist response to concerning answers, routes to immediate/local support and trusted clinical care, clear non-monitoring disclosure, and accessible behavior when offline. Do not use a negative answer as proof of safety or a positive answer as a diagnosis.

## Verification boundary

Sources were checked for this selection; access and terms may change. No permissions were requested on the user's behalf. Instruments marked deferred/unverified must not be embedded on the strength of this comparison. Initial GAD-7 wording/options/recall were checked against the University of Washington clinical form and the PHQ/GAD developer manual; PEG against the VA public-domain form and original publication. Product repeat suggestions are not medical schedules.
