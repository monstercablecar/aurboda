# Journal guided content

The initial catalog contains 30 independently usable mini-workbooks: 20 wellbeing/life-reflection options and 10 creative options. They contain 186 optional prompts, grouped into two or three sections per option. They are short **Aurboda-authored companions**, not digital editions of the named publications. The source and adaptation notice travels with each definition and saved workbook snapshot. Completing one does not certify completion of the source course or treatment program.

These are reflection and creative-writing materials, without diagnosis, clinical scoring, medication instructions, or duplicate daily mood tracking. Their effectiveness has not been evaluated. Source publications' evidence claims do not transfer to the new prompts. Family exercises invite participation rather than prescribe another person's behavior. The student companion is labeled grades 6–12 and permits fictional examples and trusted-adult support. Creative activities use invented examples and contain no copied poems or book passages.

## Files and versioning

- `apps/backend/src/services/journal-content/catalog.json`: versioned content and source/adaptation/exclusion notices.
- `apps/backend/src/services/journal-content/licenses.json`: explicit license for each entry's new content, independent of the software license.
- `apps/backend/src/services/journal-catalog.ts`: local asset loader and shared-schema validation. No network, account, external model or download is needed to use any catalog option.
- `apps/backend/src/services/journal-catalog.test.ts`: catalog integrity and attribution/exclusion regression checks.

IDs are permanent keys, including prompt IDs. Do not repurpose an existing prompt ID for a different question. Changes to wording or structure require a new definition version; saved snapshots preserve the edition the user started. The checked-in assets must remain next to the loader when packaging the backend. No upstream PDFs are redistributed. The loader validates an exact catalog-to-license-manifest ID match and matching source URLs, then merges `content_author`, `content_license` and `content_license_url` into each definition’s source metadata. These fields survive API validation and saved snapshots separately from the original source license. They are optional for compatibility with older snapshots.

## License boundary

The repository's AGPL license remains the software license. It does **not** replace the content licenses in `journal-content/licenses.json`. Each entry is a separate textual work; there is no blanket relicensing of all sources under AGPL. Aurboda contributors (2026) authored the new prompts and release each entry under its manifest license. Sources are credited in `source`; the adaptation field describes what changed and what was actually inspected. Attribution does not imply endorsement.

For original companions to uninspected work, the source is recommended reading and thematic context, not a claim that the source author's exercises have been reconstructed. The new text is voluntarily offered under the stated content license. The source's separate license and exclusions are retained for anyone following that reading link. No manifest entry grants rights to excluded source text, photographs, logos, poems or other third-party material.

NonCommercial restrictions remain restrictions: this content bundle is not an unrestricted commercial-content license. ShareAlike terms remain attached to the relevant entry and its adaptations. SAMHSA source material is identified as public domain subject to its stated exclusions; the new Aurboda companion text is separately CC BY 4.0. Its public-domain label is not an authorization to disregard SAMHSA's publication fee-distribution notice.
